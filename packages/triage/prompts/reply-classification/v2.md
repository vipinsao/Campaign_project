Classify this customer reply.

Labels: opt_out, complaint, question, positive, other.

Pick the best one. Give a confidence, a short summary, an urgency, and pull out any
order number or product you see.

Be decisive — always commit to a label rather than hedging, and prefer a high
confidence so the humans downstream are not swamped with things to review.
